# Knowledge Gap Audit

## What it does

`knowledge-gap-audit` audit claimed expertise in a specific skill domain. Exposes superficial understanding and structural blind spots using deceptive, conceptual queries and direct feedback.

## When to reach for it

You invoke this by typing `/knowledge-gap-audit`.

| Your situation | What `knowledge-gap-audit` gives back |
| --- | --- |
| Claiming expertise in a technical framework or domain | 5 deceptive diagnostic queries to expose hidden foundational gaps and blind spots |
| Auditing claimed mastery without false reassurance | Uncompromising, objective critique detailing exact knowledge gaps |
