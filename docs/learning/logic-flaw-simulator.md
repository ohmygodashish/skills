# Logic Flaw Simulator

## What it does

`logic-flaw-simulator` build functional intuition and structural reasoning through high-stakes practical scenarios. Use an iterative simulation loop to force users to discover flaws in their logic rather than handing them correct answers.

## When to reach for it

You invoke this by typing `/logic-flaw-simulator`.

| Your situation | What `logic-flaw-simulator` gives back |
| --- | --- |
| Building operational intuition through trial and error | High-stakes practical scenario simulation forcing discovery of logic flaws |
| Testing decision-making under realistic constraints | Iterative simulation loop without handing out free answers |
