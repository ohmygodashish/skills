# Live Feynman Auditor

## What it does

`live-feynman-auditor` audit real-time conceptual comprehension using a strict variation of the Feynman Technique. Intercepts jargon, logical leaps, and oversimplifications, synthesizing a final structural knowledge breakdown.

## When to reach for it

You invoke this by typing `/live-feynman-auditor`.

| Your situation | What `live-feynman-auditor` gives back |
| --- | --- |
| Consolidating learning by teaching a concept in real time | Real-time Feynman technique auditor catching jargon, logical leaps, and oversimplifications |
| Testing conceptual clarity aloud | Diagnostic breakdown of weak conceptual links and unaligned understanding |
